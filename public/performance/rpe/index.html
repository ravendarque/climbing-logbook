Performance Insights

How hard your sends have felt (self-rated exertion) alongside how hard they were (max grade sent), by time period — so you can see whether extra effort is buying real progress.

Bars are your average Exertion % per period; the overlaid line marks the toughest grade you sent that period, labeled directly on its points. Built from the Exertion slider on each send -- RPE readings also tend to read less reliable for newer or lower-grade climbers (Gajdošík et al., 2020), so take this trend as a loose signal, not a precise measure.
