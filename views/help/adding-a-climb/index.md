# Adding a climb

Open your Logbook, pick the Boulder or Sport tab, and press **Add**. The climb goes into whichever tab you're on.

## Fill in the details

1. **Problem name** (Boulder) or **Route name** (Sport). Required.
2. **Place.** Choose from your places, or pick **Add new place** (see below). Required.
3. **Grade.** Use the arrows or tap the grade to pick one. Tap the ruler icon to switch grade scale. Your choice is remembered for each discipline. Not sure how scales compare? See [Grade scales & conversion](/help/grade-scales/).
4. **Date.** Type `YYYY-MM-DD` (or just `YYYY-MM`), or use the calendar. It starts as today.
5. **Style** (Sport only). Lead or Top Rope.
6. **Status.** Flash (Boulder) or Onsight (Sport), Send (Boulder) or Redpoint (Sport), Project, Check out or Archived. It starts on Send.
7. **Notes** and a **Video URL** if you want them. The video link needs to start with `http://` or `https://`.

Press **Save & close** when you're done.

## Add a new place

Open the place list and choose **Add new place**. Enter a **Location** (required), an **Area** if you like, and a **Country**. If the location already exists, the country fills in for you. Your new place is selected straight away.

## Grade scales with number, letter and modifier

Some scales, like Font (Non-standard) and French (Non-standard), let you build a grade from three parts: a number (1–9), an optional letter (a, b or c) and an optional + or −. Use these when a guidebook grades in its own way.

## No connection?

Your climb is saved on your device and shows a **Pending sync** badge until it's sent. See [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).

## Want more detail?

With Athlete Mode on, the form gets a second page for effort, attempts, hard moves and pain. See [Athlete Mode](/help/athlete-mode/).
