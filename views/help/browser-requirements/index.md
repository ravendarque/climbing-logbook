# Browser requirements

Climbing Logbook works in any up-to-date browser, on a phone, tablet or computer. Here's what it needs.

## Browser versions

You need one of these, or newer:

- **Firefox** 128.
- **Safari** 16.4.
- **A browser built on Chromium**, such as Brave or Vivaldi: Chromium 111.

On an iPhone or iPad, browsers use Safari's engine unless they say otherwise, so it's your iOS or iPadOS version that counts: 16.4 or newer.

To find your version, open your browser's *About* page. Browsers built on Chromium list a Chromium version there as well as their own, and it's the Chromium one that counts.

## Settings it needs

- **JavaScript** turned on. The app doesn't work without it.
- **Cookies** allowed. We use one cookie, and it keeps you logged in (see [Privacy](/help/privacy/)).
- **Site storage** allowed, so your logbook works with no signal. Without it, Climbing Logbook still works online, but it downloads your logbook every time you open it (see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/)).

## Optional extras

- **Passkeys** need Safari 18.4 or Chromium 129, or newer, or iOS or iPadOS 18.4 on an iPhone or iPad. Any Firefox that runs Climbing Logbook has them. On an older browser you won't see the option, and you log in with your password instead. See [Passkeys](/help/passkeys/).
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** needs an authenticator app on your phone. See [Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](/help/two-factor-authentication/).

## On an older browser

Pages may not load, or may not look right. Update your browser. If your device can't run a new enough version, use a different browser or device.

If your browser meets all of this and something still doesn't work, [report an issue](/help/report-an-issue/).
