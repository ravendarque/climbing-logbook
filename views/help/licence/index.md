# Licence

Climbing Logbook's source code is public, on [GitHub](https://github.com/ravendarque/climbing-logbook). This is what you can do with it. The full text is in [LICENSE.md](https://github.com/ravendarque/climbing-logbook/blob/main/LICENSE.md), and it's what counts; this page is a quicker read.

<h2 id="what-you-can-do">What you can do</h2>

- Read, run and change the code.
- Share it, with or without your changes, as long as the licence goes with it.
- Use it for anything that isn't commercial: personal use, hobby projects, study, research, and charities and other non-profits.

<h2 id="what-you-cant-do">What you can't do</h2>

- **Make money from it.** You can't run it, or anything based on it, as a paid service, sell it as part of a product, or show adverts with it.
- **Use it if you're excluded.** The licence isn't granted to:
  - governments, armed forces and state bodies of states the International Court of Justice has found in breach, or at plausible risk of breach, of the Genocide Convention or of international humanitarian or human rights law
  - anyone with an outstanding, public arrest warrant from the International Criminal Court
  - any organisation that has someone like that on its staff, board or payroll, or as an adviser or patron
  - companies that materially support those states' conduct
  - anyone acting for any of them

These are judgements about governments, the people who lead them and the organisations that support them, never about the people who live in those countries. [LICENSE-SCHEDULE.md](https://github.com/ravendarque/climbing-logbook/blob/main/LICENSE-SCHEDULE.md) lists who's covered at the moment, and the court decisions behind each entry.

The same people and organisations can't use Climbing Logbook itself either (see [Terms of use](/help/terms/#who-cant-use)).

<h2 id="no-warranty">No warranty</h2>

The code comes as it is, with no warranty, like almost all open-source code.
