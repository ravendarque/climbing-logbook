# Deleting an entry

Deleting is permanent. There's no undo and no trash.

1. Press the pencil icon on the climb you want to remove.
2. Press **Delete entry** at the bottom of the form.
3. Confirm when asked.

If you're offline, the row stays in your log with a **Pending delete** badge and a line through its name. It disappears once your device syncs. If you delete a climb you added offline before it ever synced, it's simply removed.

Just want it out of the way? Set its status to **Archived** instead. See [Editing an entry](/help/editing-an-entry/).
