# Beta channel

The beta gives you early access to new features before they're released to everyone. Beta features may have bugs.

The beta uses **the same logbook and the same data** as the main app. Anything you add, change or delete in the beta is real, and shows up in the main app too.

## Joining the beta

1. Open the menu and go to **My account**.
2. Choose **Beta channel**.
3. Read what changes, then choose **Join the beta**.

You're taken to your log in the beta, at **beta.climbinglogbook.com**. Each device downloads your logbook the first time it opens the beta, so do that while you have a connection.

## Using the beta in a browser

- **Logging in at climbinglogbook.com takes you to the beta** while you're enrolled.
- **my.climbinglogbook.com always opens the main app**, enrolled or not. Use it whenever you want the main app.

## Using the beta as an app

The beta is a separate app called **Logbook Beta**, with its own icon marked *Beta*. You can keep both Logbook and Logbook Beta installed, and each opens its own version.

To install Logbook Beta, go to **beta.climbinglogbook.com** while you're enrolled:

- **Android (Chrome):** open the menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

You may need to log in inside the app the first time you open it.

## Before you switch, sync

Changes you make offline wait on the device you made them on, in the app you made them in, until they sync. Before you join or leave the beta, make sure every device you've used offline has synced: open the menu and check there's no *Offline* or *Syncing…* status. It's the same check as in [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).

The Beta channel page tells you if the app you're using has changes waiting on this device.

## Leaving the beta

1. Open the menu and go to **My account**.
2. Choose **Beta channel**.
3. Choose **Leave the beta**.

You're taken back to your log in the main app. From then on, Logbook Beta shows a message saying you're not enrolled, so you can uninstall it.

Anything you created with beta-only features stays in your logbook, but the main app may not show it until those features are released.

## Using both at once

Having the main app and the beta open at the same time works like using two devices: each keeps its own copy of your logbook on the device, and a change made in one appears in the other once it has synced.

Related: [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).
