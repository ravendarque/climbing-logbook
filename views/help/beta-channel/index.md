# Check our beta

The beta gives you early access to new features before they're released to everyone. Beta features may have bugs.

The beta uses your logbook, not a copy of it. Anything you add, change or delete in the beta is real, and shows up outside the beta too.

## Joining the beta

1. Open the menu and go to **My account**.
2. Choose **Check our beta**.
3. Confirm that you understand what changes and what you need to do by hitting **Join the beta**.

You're taken to your log in the beta, at **beta.climbinglogbook.com**. Each device downloads your logbook the first time it opens the beta, so do that while you have a connection.

## Getting back to the regular version

While you're in the beta:

- **Logging in at climbinglogbook.com takes you to the beta.**
- **my.climbinglogbook.com always opens the regular version**, without the beta features.

Use my.climbinglogbook.com when you want Climbing Logbook without the beta features: for example, if a beta feature isn't working for you and you need to log a climb, or to compare how something looks with and without a new feature. It's the same logbook either way.

## Installing Logbook Beta

If you use Climbing Logbook as an installed Progressive Web App (PWA), the beta is a separate PWA called **Logbook Beta**, with a yellow *Beta* tag on its icon. You can keep both installed, and each opens its own version.

Install it the same way as Climbing Logbook, from **beta.climbinglogbook.com** while you're in the beta. [Installing the PWA](/help/install/) has the steps for every device.

## Before you switch, sync

Changes you make offline wait on the device you made them on, in the version you made them in, until they sync. Before you join or leave the beta, make sure every device you've used offline has synced: open the menu and check there's no *Offline* or *Syncing…* status. It's the same check as in [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).

The Check our beta page tells you if the version you're using has changes waiting on this device.

## Leaving the beta

1. Open the menu and go to **My account**.
2. Choose **Check our beta**.
3. Choose **Leave the beta**.

You're taken back to your log in the regular version. From then on, Logbook Beta shows a message saying you're not in the beta, so you can uninstall it.

Anything you created with beta-only features stays in your logbook, but the regular version may not show it until those features are released.

## Using both at once

Having the regular version and the beta open at the same time works like using two devices: each keeps its own copy of your logbook on the device, and a change made in one appears in the other once it has synced.

Related: [Installing the PWA](/help/install/), [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).
