# Describing the beta

In Athlete Mode, the Performance page of the entry form asks for the hardest move or sequence on each climb. Write it the way you'd describe it to a friend, and Climbing Logbook works out what you mean.

> Right heel hook on the lip, cross left hand to a sidepull crimp, then deadpoint to the pinch. Crux!

## Choose the wall angle first

Choose the angle where the hardest move is: slab, vert, overhang, roof, chimney or dihedral. It's needed on every climb except a Checkout, because your Strengths / Weaknesses insights compare your climbing angle by angle.

The wall angle is never picked for you. If your beta names a different angle, for example "steep" on a climb you've marked as vert, the read-back underlines the word and asks you to check which is right.

## How it reads your beta

As you type, **How it was read** shows your words coloured by what each one was read as:

- the terrain, such as steep or slab;
- holds and features, such as a crimp, sloper, jug, lip or arête;
- the angle of a hold, such as a sidepull, undercling or gaston;
- positions, such as a heel hook, drop knee or knee bar;
- moves, such as a crossover, deadpoint, dyno or mantle;
- which hand or foot, such as "left hand" or "rf".

Open **Read as steps** to see your beta written out step by step, with the crux marked if you said "crux".

It understands common slang and shorthand, like "bucket" for a jug or "lh" for left hand, and it fixes obvious typos, so "slopper" is read as sloper.

## When it isn't sure

A word it isn't sure about is underlined, with a button underneath.

- **A word with more than one meaning,** like "jam" (a hand jam or a foot jam). Choose the one you meant and the word is changed in your text, so it's read the same way every time.
- **A word it doesn't know.** It suggests the closest word it does know. Use the suggestion, or keep the word as you wrote it.

Your text is always saved exactly as it is. Words it doesn't know are kept, they just don't count towards your insights.

## Tips

- Name the hand or foot when it matters: "left hand", "right foot", "lf".
- Use "from", "to" and "on" for holds: "from the undercling to the crimp".
- In a longer sequence, put "crux" straight after the move that is: "deadpoint to the pinch, crux".

## Where it's used

Your wall angles and hardest moves are what [Strengths / Weaknesses](/help/performance-insights/) is built from. Something only counts as a weakness when it's hard on climbs below your level, so climbing a lot of it won't make it look weak.

Like the rest of your Athlete Mode details, your beta is private. It's never shown on your public logbook.
