# Privacy

We don't sell your data and we don't share it for advertising. Here's what we keep and why.

## Who we are

Climbing Logbook is an independent project run from the UK. Under data protection law, we're the controller of your data, which means we're responsible for looking after it. For anything about your data, [contact us](/help/report-an-issue/).

## What we keep

- **Your account.** Your username, your email address and your password. We can't read your password, because it's hashed (scrambled in a way that can't be reversed). If you turn on [Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)](/help/two-factor-authentication/), we also keep its setup key and your backup codes, encrypted, until you turn it off or delete your account. If you add a [passkey](/help/passkeys/), we keep its public key, which is no use on its own, until you remove it or delete your account. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device.
- **Your logbook.** The climbs, places and settings you add.
- **Health information**, only if you agree to the Health Data Agreement. See [Health information](#health-data).

We use this to run the app for you, and for nothing else.

## Why we use it

- **To run your account and logbook.** This is the service you signed up for, so it's covered by our agreement with you, the [Terms of use](/help/terms/). It covers your account, your climbs, places and settings, and the emails we send about your account.
- **Health information,** only if you agree to the [Health Data Agreement](#health-data). If you don't, we don't collect any. You can withdraw at any time.
- **To keep Climbing Logbook secure and working.** Request logs, rate limits and bot checks protect everyone's logbook. They never hold your password, your notes or your health information.
- **To see which pages get used,** through visit statistics that don't use cookies.
- **To answer your feedback and reports,** and to act on reports about content.

## How long we keep it

- **Email links**, for checking your email address or resetting your password, are deleted within a day of expiring.
- **Your account and logbook** stay until you delete your account in My account, which removes them. Our backups drop them within 30 days.

## Who can see your logbook

You choose when you set up your account, and you can change it at any time. In My account, the **Public Logbook** setting controls whether anyone can view your logbook and map. Your Grade Pyramid, Injury insights and other Performance Insights are always private to you.

We only look at a logbook to investigate a report, misuse, or a problem you've asked us to help with, and every look is logged.

<h2 id="health-data">Health information</h2>

Health data is off until you agree to the Health Data Agreement in My account. Data protection law treats health information as sensitive, so we ask you first. The rest of Climbing Logbook works the same either way.

- **What counts as health information:** pain and injuries, which phase of your menstrual cycle you're in (by phase only, never dates), and your energy and mood, when you choose to log them.
- **What it's for:** finding patterns in your climbing performance, shown only in your own Performance insights.
- **Who can turn it on:** you need to be 16 or over, or have your parent or guardian's agreement.
- **Where it goes:** it's stored with the rest of your logbook. It's never shown on your public profile and is only used for your own reports. A site administrator can see it for support and moderation, and for nothing else.
- **Turning it on and off:** go to My account, then *Health data*. Turning it off deletes all your health information. Your climbs and other performance insights data are unaffected.
- **How long we keep it:** until you turn it off or delete your account. After that, it's removed from our backups within 30 days.

Your other devices remove their copy the next time they sync.

## Cookies

We use one cookie, and it keeps you logged in. The app needs it to work, so there's no cookie banner. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

## On your device

So that Climbing Logbook opens and works with no signal, each device you use it on keeps a copy of your logbook in the browser's storage:

- your climbs, including notes, moves and pain flags;
- your places, locations and settings;
- any changes that haven't synced yet;
- the app itself, so its pages open offline.

It's kept under your account. Someone else who logs in on the same browser can't see it in Climbing Logbook.

**Logging out** removes the saved app pages, but keeps your logbook and any unsynced changes on the device, so nothing you logged offline is lost. They sync the next time you log in there.

**To remove everything from a device,** go to My account and choose *Clear device data*. It logs you out. Nothing is deleted from your account, and your logbook syncs back the next time you log in on that device. If changes are still waiting to sync, a warning says so first, because clearing the device would lose them. Do this before handing a device back if you've used someone else's.

You can also clear Climbing Logbook's site data in your browser's settings. Check first that the menu shows no *Offline* or *Syncing…* status, or changes that haven't synced will be lost.

If your browser or an extension stops Climbing Logbook storing your logbook, it still works online, but it downloads your logbook every time you open it and won't work offline. If it blocks cookies too, you won't be able to log in. [Working offline](/help/working-offline/) has more on using it at the crag, and [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/) covers what to do if something looks wrong.

## Visit statistics

We count visits to see which pages get used and what devices people use. It doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't follow you around or build a profile of you.

## Trends and reporting

We may look at anonymised, combined figures across all users, like how many climbs are logged in total. They never point back to you.

## Sharing

We only share what's needed to run the app, and only for that:

- **our hosting and database provider**, which runs the app and stores your logbook;
- **our email providers**, which send the emails about your account and receive messages sent to our support address;
- **our bot-check provider**, which checks sign-ups are from a person.

They only use your data to provide their service to us. Some of them process data outside the UK, including in the US. Those transfers are covered by the UK–US data bridge, and by the UK's standard contract terms for international transfers.

## Your rights

You can:

- **see and download your logbook**: My account, then *Export entries* (see [Import and export](/help/import-and-export/));
- **correct it**: [edit any climb](/help/editing-an-entry/), or your details in My account;
- **delete it**: My account, then *Delete account*. That deletes your account and everything in it;
- **withdraw your health data agreement**: My account, then *Health data*;
- **ask for anything else**, like a copy of everything we hold about you, or to restrict or object to how we use it: [contact us](/help/report-an-issue/).

We reply as soon as we can, and within 28 days at most.

## Automated decisions

We don't make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Your Performance insights are only for your own information.

## Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

<h2 id="ai-training">AI training</h2>

Your logbook isn't used to train AI models. We block AI crawlers and bots, and tell them they may not use anything here for AI training. We don't use it for AI training ourselves either.
