# Editing an entry

Press the pencil icon at the end of a row in your Logbook. The same form you used to add the climb opens, filled in with what you saved.

Change anything you like: the name, place, grade, date, style, status, notes and video. With Athlete Mode on, you can change the extra details on the second page too. Press **Save & close** to keep your changes.

You can't move a climb between Boulder and Sport. If you logged it in the wrong tab, [delete it](/help/deleting-an-entry/) and add it again in the right one.

If you're offline, your edit is saved on your device and syncs later. See [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).
