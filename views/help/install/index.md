# Installing the PWA

Climbing Logbook is a Progressive Web App (PWA): a website you can install on your phone, tablet or computer. Once installed, it gets its own icon and opens in its own window, without the browser's address bar. It doesn't come from an app store; you install it from your browser.

Installing it is the most reliable way to use Climbing Logbook offline, and on iPhone and iPad it's the only reliable way (see [Working offline](/help/working-offline/)).

## Before you install

Log in at **climbinglogbook.com**. You'll land on your log, and that's the page to install from.

## Android

**Chrome:** open the menu (⋮) and choose **Install app**. On some versions it's called **Add to Home screen**.

Other Android browsers may not install it fully as a PWA, so use Chrome if you can.

## iPhone and iPad

1. In **Safari**, tap **Share** (the square with an arrow).
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**. If you see **Open as Web App**, leave it switched on.
3. Tap **Add**.

On iOS 16.4 and later you can do the same from Chrome or Edge: tap **Share** in the address bar, then **Add to Home Screen**.

The first time you open it from your home screen, you may need to log in again.

## Computers

- **Chrome or Edge** (Windows, Mac, Linux, ChromeOS): click the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar, or open the browser menu and look for **Install**. Confirm, and it opens in its own window.
- **Safari on a Mac** (macOS Sonoma or later): choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
- **Firefox on Windows** (version 143 or later): click **Add tab to taskbar** in the address bar.
- **Firefox on a Mac or Linux** can't install it. Use Chrome, Edge or Safari instead.

Browsers change their menus from time to time, so the exact wording may differ slightly from what's written here.

## Logbook Beta

If you've joined the beta, Logbook Beta is a separate PWA. Install it the same way, from **beta.climbinglogbook.com**. You can keep both installed.

## Removing it

Remove it like any other app on your device. On a computer, open it, then open its menu (⋮ or ⋯) and choose **Uninstall**. Removing it doesn't delete your logbook. Your climbs are in your account.

Any changes that haven't synced yet are only on that device, so check they've synced first: open the menu and make sure there's no *Offline* or *Syncing…* status.

## Updates

It updates itself. New versions download in the background while you're online and take effect the next time you open it.

Related: [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).
