# Quick start

You can be logging climbs in a couple of minutes.

1. **Try it first.** On the home page, pick a demo logbook to look around without signing up.
2. **Create your account.** Register with your invite code, then log in.
3. **Add a climb.** Open your Logbook, pick Boulder or Sport, and press **Add**. See [Adding a climb](/help/adding-a-climb/). Made a mistake? [Edit it](/help/editing-an-entry/) or [delete it](/help/deleting-an-entry/).
4. **Keep climbing.** Your climbs save on your device straight away and sync when you're back online. See [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).
5. **Already have a logbook elsewhere?** [Import it](/help/import-and-export/) from a spreadsheet.
6. **See your progress.** Switch on [Athlete Mode](/help/athlete-mode/) for [Performance Insights](/help/performance-insights/).
7. **Check a grade.** Not sure how it compares between scales? See [Grade scales & conversion](/help/grade-scales/).
