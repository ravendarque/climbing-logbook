# Quick start

You can be logging climbs in a couple of minutes.

1. **Try it first.** On the home page, pick a demo logbook to look around without signing up.
2. **Create your account.** Register with your invite code, then log in.
3. **Log a climb.** Open your log and add an entry: the climb, its grade, and how it went.
4. **Keep climbing.** Entries save on your device straight away and sync when you're back online. See [Working offline](/help/working-offline/).
5. **Check your grades.** Not sure how a grade compares between scales? See [Grade scales & conversion](/help/grade-scales/).

More detail on each of these is on its way. Use the search box to find a topic.
