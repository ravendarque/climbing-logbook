# Terms of use

These are the rules for using Climbing Logbook. When you create an account, you agree to them.

Climbing Logbook is an independent project run from the UK. If anything here isn't clear, email **support@climbinglogbook.com**.

<h2 id="your-account">Your account</h2>

- Online privacy law means you need to be 13 or over to create an account. If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian to set one up for you.
- You're responsible for what happens on your account, so keep your password safe.
- You can delete your account at any time from My account, along with everything in it.

<h2 id="your-logbook">Your logbook</h2>

What you log is yours. You give us permission to store it and to show it to you, and, if your logbook is public, to show it to anyone who visits it. That's all we use it for. We never sell it or use it for advertising (see [Privacy](/help/privacy/)).

Your logbook starts private. If you make it public, in setup or later in My account, anyone can see it, including people who don't have an account. You can make it private again at any time.

<h2 id="what-we-stand-for">What we stand for</h2>

Climbing should be for everyone, and Climbing Logbook is for everyone who climbs. We're openly left-leaning, and we won't host bigotry, whether it's shouted or hidden in jokes and dog whistles. There is no "both sides" here: you can't use Climbing Logbook to promote:

- racism, antisemitism, Islamophobia or xenophobia
- Zionism
- misogyny or sexism
- homophobia, biphobia or transphobia, including misgendering or deadnaming someone on purpose
- ableism
- fascism, or the glorification of violence against any group

<h2 id="acceptable-use">Acceptable use</h2>

Keep what you post about climbing. Names, notes and video links on a public logbook are seen by others, so don't use them for:

- **Anything illegal**, or links to it. That includes terrorist content and any sexual content involving children, which we remove straight away and report to the authorities.
- **Harassment**, threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- **Other people's personal details**, such as their address, phone number or photos, without their permission.
- **Sexual or graphic content.**
- **Spam, adverts or scams**, or links to anything malicious. Video links can only come from YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram, TikTok or Facebook, to prevent spam or malicious links.
- **Pretending to be someone else.**

And because it matters to climbing: please respect access agreements and seasonal restrictions, such as bird nesting bans, and don't use your logbook to encourage anyone to break them.

<h2 id="reporting">Reporting something</h2>

If you see something on a public logbook that breaks these terms, use **Report this logbook** in its menu, or **Report this entry** from an entry's notes. Reports go to a real person. We try to deal with illegal content within 24 hours, and with everything else within 7 days.

<h2 id="what-happens">What happens if the rules are broken</h2>

We look at each case and do what fits it:

- **Hide a climb** from your public logbook. You still see it in your own logbook.
- **Suspend your account**, which signs you out and hides your public logbook until we lift it.
- **Delete your account** and everything in it.
- **Ban you**: delete your account, and stop your email address and username being used again.

Illegal content and hate are removed straight away. For anything else, we usually start with the lightest step that fixes it.

<h2 id="hidden-climbs">Why is one of my climbs hidden?</h2>

Someone reported it, and we found that something in it, usually its notes or video link, breaks these terms. It's still in your logbook, and only you can see it. It's just not on your public logbook.

To get it back, change whatever broke the rules and [ask us to look again](/help/report-an-issue/). If you think we got it wrong, tell us the same way. We'll review it and reply.

<h2 id="complaints">Complaints and appeals</h2>

If you disagree with something we've done, or with how we handled a report, email **support@climbinglogbook.com** or use [Report an issue](/help/report-an-issue/). A person will review it, and we'll try to reply within 14 days.

<h2 id="our-side">Our side of it</h2>

- Climbing Logbook is free, and we provide it as it is. We work hard to keep your logbook safe, but we can't promise the app will always be available or free of bugs. Export your logbook from time to time (My account, Export).
- We might change or stop parts of the app. If we ever close it, we'll give you as much notice as we can to export your logbook.
- Climbing is dangerous. Grades, notes and logbooks are records, not safety advice.
- Nothing here takes away your rights under consumer law.
- These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
