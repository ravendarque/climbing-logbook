# Working offline

You can open Climbing Logbook and log, edit and delete climbs with no signal. It saves everything on your device first, then syncs it when you're back online.

## Before you head to the crag

Open your logbook once on the device you'll take with you, while you're signed in and online. That one visit does two things:

- **It downloads your logbook** onto the device. The first time you open it there, you'll see it syncing.
- **It saves the whole app** onto the device: your log, the map and performance pages, and your account pages. You don't need to visit each one first. On a slow connection this can take a minute after the page has loaded, so leave it open for a moment.

After that you can close the app, switch on airplane mode, and open it again. It opens straight from your device.

## Install the app

Installing Climbing Logbook puts it on your home screen, and it opens like any other app. It's the most reliable way to use it offline, and on iPhone it's the only reliable way.

- **Android (Chrome):** open climbinglogbook.com, sign in, then open the menu (⋮) and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** open climbinglogbook.com, sign in, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. You may need to sign in again inside the app the first time you open it.

**On iPhone, use the home-screen app at the crag, not a Safari tab.** Safari can delete a website's saved data if you haven't opened it for a week. It doesn't do that to home-screen apps.

**Installed Climbing Logbook before 25 September 2026?** Remove it from your home screen and install it again, so it opens your own logbook and works offline as described here.

## What happens when you're offline

- **Your changes are saved straight away.** Add a climb, edit it or delete it and it appears in your log immediately, connection or not.
- **Places you add work the same way.** A new place is saved on your device and synced later.
- **Your changes wait in a queue.** They're sent to your account, in order, as soon as your connection comes back. You don't need to do anything.
- **The menu shows what's going on.** Open the menu and look for the status: *Offline* means your changes are waiting, *Syncing…* means they're being sent. When nothing shows, you're up to date.

## Updates

New versions of Climbing Logbook download in the background while you're online. The update takes effect the next time you open the app, so it never changes underneath you while you're logging.

## What won't work offline

- **Logging in, logging out and registering.** These need a connection. Log in before you go.
- **Getting your logbook onto a new device.** The first time you sign in on a device, it has to download your logbook, so do that online.
- **Seeing changes from another device.** Something you logged on your phone won't appear on your laptop until both have synced.
- **Performance Insights reports.** They're worked out on the server, so each report shows a message asking you to connect.
- **The map.** The world map is downloaded when you first open it. If you haven't opened the Map tab online before, you'll see a message asking you to connect.
- **Importing and exporting entries, changing account details and resetting your password.** These need a connection.
- **Other people's public logbooks.** You'll need a connection to view them.

## Logging out

Logging out removes the saved copy of the app from the device, so the next person to use it can't open your pages offline. Any changes that haven't synced yet are kept, and they sync the next time you sign in. To use the app offline again, sign in and open it once while you're online.

## If you clear your browser data

Changes that haven't synced yet only exist on your device. If you clear your browser's site data before they sync, they're gone. Check the menu shows no *Offline* or *Syncing…* status before you clear anything.
