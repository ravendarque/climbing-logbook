# Performance Insights

Performance Insights turns what you log into six reports on your Performance tab. You need [Athlete Mode](/help/athlete-mode/) switched on to see it, and the reports are only ever visible to you.

They follow the discipline you're on, Boulder or Sport, and they need a connection to load.

## The reports

- **Grade Pyramid.** How your sends from the last 12 months stack up across four grade tiers, against a target shape of 8, 4, 2 and 1 climbs from the bottom up. Built from the grade of each send.
- **Volume / Intensity.** How many sends you log each period, with a line for the hardest grade you sent. Built from your sends.
- **Send / Flash Gap** (Boulder) or **Redpoint / Onsight Gap** (Sport). How your best first-attempt grade compares with your best eventual send, and how many attempts each send takes. Built from Attempts and Flash.
- **Effort / RPE Trend.** How hard your sends felt against how hard they were. Built from Exertion. It needs at least 5 sends in the period.
- **Strengths / Weaknesses.** The limb, hold, movement and wall-angle combinations you find hardest. Built from Move difficulty. A combination needs at least 3 tagged moves before it shows.
- **Injury / Pain Log.** Climbs where you flagged pain, newest first. Once a combination has at least 3 tags, it points out the pattern. It's for spotting patterns and isn't medical advice.

## Choose the period and grade scale

Volume / Intensity, the gap report and Effort / RPE have a period picker: 12 weeks (the default), 52 weeks or a custom range. The Grade Pyramid always uses the last 12 months. Most reports let you pick which grade scale to show. See [Grade scales & conversion](/help/grade-scales/).

## Not enough data?

If a report says there isn't enough data yet, keep logging. Each report's page says which detail it's built from, and every report ends with the sources behind it.
