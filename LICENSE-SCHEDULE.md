# Schedule to the Climbing Logbook Noncommercial Licence

This schedule lists who the licensor considers covered by the licence's Excluded Licensees section as at **11 October 2026**. It is for information only: the section itself decides who is covered, at the time of use, whether or not they are listed here.

## Item 1: states

The governments, armed forces and state bodies of:

| State | International Court of Justice decision |
|---|---|
| Israel | Provisional measures orders of 26 January, 28 March and 24 May 2024 under the Genocide Convention (*South Africa v. Israel*); advisory opinion of 19 July 2024 on Israel's policies and practices in the Occupied Palestinian Territory |
| Russian Federation | Provisional measures order of 16 March 2022 under the Genocide Convention (*Ukraine v. Russian Federation*); judgment of 31 January 2024 finding breaches of the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination (*Ukraine v. Russian Federation*) |
| Myanmar | Provisional measures order of 23 January 2020 under the Genocide Convention (*The Gambia v. Myanmar*) |
| Syrian Arab Republic | Provisional measures order of 16 November 2023 under the Convention against Torture (*Canada and the Netherlands v. Syrian Arab Republic*) |
| Armenia | Provisional measures order of 7 December 2021 under the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination (*Azerbaijan v. Armenia*) |
| Azerbaijan | Provisional measures orders of 7 December 2021, 22 February 2023 and 17 November 2023 under the Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination (*Armenia v. Azerbaijan*) |

## Item 2: individuals

Everyone listed as at large on the International Criminal Court's case pages (https://www.icc-cpi.int/cases), under a warrant of arrest that has been made public.

## Items 3 and 5: organisations, and those acting for them

Any organisation with an individual from item 2 in any of the roles item 3 lists, and anyone acting on behalf of a person or organisation in items 1 to 4. These are not listed by name.

## Item 4: companies

Every business enterprise in the most recent update of the United Nations database of business enterprises involved in the activities described in paragraph 96 of the report of the independent international fact-finding mission on Israeli settlements (Human Rights Council resolution 31/36), published by the Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights.
